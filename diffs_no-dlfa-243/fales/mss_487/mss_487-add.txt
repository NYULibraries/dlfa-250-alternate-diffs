     <field name="bioghist_teim">Melissa Spore (1948-2015) grew up in the Northern Virginia suburbs. She attended Marlboro College in Vermont, and Columbia University. She moved to Canada in 1974, eventually teaching instructional design in both Toronto and Saskatoon through the 1980s and 1990s. In 2013, she co-authored The Designer's Guide to Presenting Numbers, Figures, and Charts with her sister, Sally Bigwood. She passed away in Saskatoon, May 2015.</field>
