     <field name="bioghist_teim">Phoebe Dayton Gardiner Horsford was born on August 13, 1826 to Samuel Smith Gardiner and Mary Catherine L'Hommedieu Gardiner. With sister Mary Gardiner Horsford, Phoebe was privately tutored until she attended Albany Female Academy and met Eben Norton Horsford. Phoebe was an accomplished seamstress and married Eben Norton Horsford on July 22, 1857, after his first wife, her sister Mary, passed away just two years earlier. Eben and Phoebe produced one daughter, "Nellie" (Cornelia Conway Felton Horsford). Phoebe passed away October 8, 1900 in Cambridge, Massachusetts.</field>
