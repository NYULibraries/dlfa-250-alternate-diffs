     <field name="bioghist_teim">Mary Catherine L'Hommedieu Gardiner (July 6, 1806 - January 28, 1838) was the only daughter of Ezra and Mary L'Hommedieu. She married Samuel Smith Gardiner in 1823 and gave birth to three daughters: Mary L'Hommedieu Gardiner in 1824, Phoebe Dayton Gardiner in 1826, and Frances Eliza Gardiner (Fanny) in 1832. Mary and Phoebe would both marry Eben Norton Horsford, whose personal and professional life are documented in Record Group IV: Horsford Family. Frances married George Martin Lane, a Harvard professor of Latin Language and Literature, in 1857.</field>
