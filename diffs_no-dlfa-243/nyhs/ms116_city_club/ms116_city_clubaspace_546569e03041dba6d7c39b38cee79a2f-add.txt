The volumes of Miscellaneous Committee Minutes contain the minutes of various committees, special committees and sub-committees. There are variations in how committee names are presented. The topics of committees include city affairs, legislation, city planning and development, finance, public health, nominations, courts, parks, education, housing, transit and transportation, taxation, port development, employment, the re-chartering of New York, and the House Committee.
