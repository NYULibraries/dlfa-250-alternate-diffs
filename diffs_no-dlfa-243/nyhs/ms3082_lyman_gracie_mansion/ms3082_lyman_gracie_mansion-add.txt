     <field name="bioghist_teim">Susan Elizabeth Lyman was born in New York City on November 18, 1905. She graduated from Smith College in 1929 and received a MA from Columbia University in 1933. She was curator of manuscripts at the New-York Historical Society from 1939 to 1946, and from 1949 to 1959, she worked at the Museum of the City of New York, helping to develop the "Please Touch" room for children. Lyman was also a writer and authority on the history of New York City, publishing "The Face of New York" (1955), with photographs by Andreas Feininger; "Young Folks' New York" (1960); and "The Story of New York" (1966). She also published "Lady Historian: Martha J. Lamb" in 1969. Lyman died in 1976 from injuries received in an automobile accident.</field>
