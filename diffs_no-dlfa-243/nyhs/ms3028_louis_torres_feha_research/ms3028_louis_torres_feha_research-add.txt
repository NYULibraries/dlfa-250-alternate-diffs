+    <field name="bioghist_teim">Louis Torres (1921-2017) was a senior historian at the National Park Service (NPS). In the late 1950s, he began his NPS career at the Federal Hall National Historic Site where he was appointed to research and write both a construction history of the Customs House/Sub-Treasury/Federal Hall National Memorial, and a Historic Structures Report. Torres's work contributed to the rehabilitation of Federal Hall, beginning in the early 1960s. He became site manager for Federal Hall, running its daily operations, events and public outreach activities. Although Torres left NPS for a time to become a historian with the U.S. Air Force, he returned in 1973, eventually retiring in 1981.</field>
