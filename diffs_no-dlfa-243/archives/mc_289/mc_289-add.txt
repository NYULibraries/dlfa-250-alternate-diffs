     <field name="bioghist_teim">The firm of Parmer, Herzog, and Chadwick was established by Robert L. Herzog in 1926 as a bond trading and brokerage company. The New York area company suffered losses in the stock market crash of 1929 but stayed in business by trading in foreign bonds. Over the years, the firm evolved into Herzog, Heine, Geduld, Inc., becoming a member of the New York Stock Exchange, performing retail brokerage, clearing, bond brokerage and limited investment banking and related financial services. The company was acquired by Merrill Lynch in 2000.</field>
