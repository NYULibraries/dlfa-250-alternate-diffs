     <field name="scopecontent_teim">The Hasia Diner Papers collection is divided into one series which is broken down into eight subseries. Each subseries represents one of the books that Hasia Diner wrote. The material in this collection consists of research materials that Professor Diner used when writing about American Jewish history, American immigration history, and American women's history. Almost all of the material is photocopies of primary and secondary sources. Majority of the folder titles in the collection were transferred from Diner's original research folders.</field>
