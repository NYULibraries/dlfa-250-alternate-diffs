     <field name="bioghist_teim">Raymond J. Love was born July 10, 1919 in Manhattan. He was one of eleven children of Sarah and John Love. He married Catherine Stanton in 1951 and they had five children (Kathryn, Patrick, Joan, John, and Nora). He worked for the Pennsylvania Railroad for 20 years, from 1949 to 1969, on the midnight to 8a.m. shift at Sunnyside Yards in Queens. During this period he also worked construction throughout the NYC metro area. One project Raymond Love worked on was the construction of the Verrazano-Narrows Bridge, building the roadways near the end of the construction project. He retired in 1985 and passed away at the age of 84 on November 6, 2003.</field>
