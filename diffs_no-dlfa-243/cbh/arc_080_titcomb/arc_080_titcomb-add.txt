     <field name="bioghist_teim">Mary A. Titcomb was born December 30, 1830. She attended Mount Holyoke Female Seminary, now Mount Holyoke College, in South Hadley, MA and graduated in 1850. Following graduation, Titcomb began her career as a teacher and writer. From 1850-1856, Titcomb taught at Mount Holyoke Female Seminary. She then taught at Rutgers Female Institute, in New York City, until 1861. From 1865-1881, Titcomb wrote several weekly magazine series for Harper &amp; Brothers, publishers of Harper's Weekly and Harper's Bazar (now called Harper's Bazaar). "Mrs. Typeset's Diary" (1867-1870), a fictional series, was published in Harper's Bazar. Titcomb also wrote two weekly news and gossip series for Harper &amp; Brothers. "Saying and Doings" (1870-1881) was published in Harper's Bazar and "Home and Foreign Gossip" (1865-1881) was published in Harper's Weekly. Titcomb died in 1910.</field>
