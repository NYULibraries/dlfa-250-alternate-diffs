     <field name="scopecontent_teim">The collection contains correspondence, genealogy notes, school papers, commencement programs, a deed, calling cards, religious writings, obituaries, wills, birth certificates, death certificates, marriage certificates, and citizenship papers pertaining to the Aschner and Jacobson families of Brooklyn Heights and Montgomery, N.Y. Documents in the collection belonging to or created by Fred Hoyt chronicle his research into his family's history in Brooklyn. Earlier letters from the Aschner family are written in German, and religious documents are written in Hebrew.</field>
     <field name="scopecontent_teim">Photographs in the collection include framed portraits, cabinet cards, cartes-de-visite, tintypes, and both black-and-white and color prints of the Wachsmann, Aschner, Jacobson, and Hoyt families.
