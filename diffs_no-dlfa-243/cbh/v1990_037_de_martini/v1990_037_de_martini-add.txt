     <field name="bioghist_teim">Celestine De Martini was a resident of Carroll Gardens neighborhood of Brooklyn, and worked as a civilian employee of the United States Navy at the Brooklyn Navy Yard (officially known as the New York Naval Shipyard) during the Second World War. Her father, Anthony De Martini, served as a Deputy Sheriff and was involved in numerous civic and political organizations including the Italian American Civic Union. He also served as alternate delegate to the Republican National Convention in the 1920s. In the 1930s, the De Martini family lived at 73 First Place in Carroll Gardens.</field>
