     <field name="bioghist_teim">The inaugural Third Kings Masonic Camp Fund Annual Entertainment and Dance fundraiser was held in 1931. The event included speeches, entertainment, and dancing and for many years was held at the Brooklyn Academy of Music. Each year, the Fund would publish an event program (sometimes referred to as the Journal) listing officers and members of event committees, donors and advertisers, and all participating Masonic lodges.</field>
