     <field name="bioghist_teim">Laura Fitzpatrick was born on October 25, 1927 in Mount Meigs, Alabama. Her family moved to New York when she was ten years old and she started taking photographs with an Agfa Billy camera soon after. Fitzpatrick used the rooftop of her apartment building at 635 Broadway in Williamsburg like a photo studio, taking photographs of friends, family, and neighbors. She married Ernest Evans in 1951 and they had four children, including the donor of this collection, Daniel Evans.</field>
     <field name="bioghist_teim">Fitzpatrick received her nursing degree from the Young Women's Christian Association (YWCA) School of Practical Nursing at 30 Third Avenue in Brooklyn and she worked at Brookdale Hospital for 33 years. She went back to school in the 1970s for her Registered Nursing (RN) degree.</field>
