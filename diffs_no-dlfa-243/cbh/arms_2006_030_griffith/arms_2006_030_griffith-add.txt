Highlights of the collection include correspondence between William Patton Griffith and the general public, constituents, and political colleagues, such as letters to and from the office of Franklin Roosevelt, then Governor of New York.      <field name="bioghist_teim">After his military service, Griffith went on to pursue a career as an editor at The Pittsburgh Dispatch, The New York Herald, The World, The Brooklyn Eagle, and The Brooklyn Press. Griffith later moved on to public service in Brooklyn, working as a court reporter in the Kings County Supreme Court and serving as the deputy City Clerk of Brooklyn. He was also an active member of the Kings County Republican Committee and served as a delegate to national and State encampments of the GAR, as well as an aide to the staffs of GAR Commanders-in-Chief, department commanders, and local grand marshals.</field>
