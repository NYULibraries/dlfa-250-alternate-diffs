Members of the Brooklyn League served on committees concerned with various civic and social issues in Brooklyn, including Education, Gas and Electricity, Municipal Ordinances, Legislation, Assessment and Taxation, etc. The Brooklyn League's headquarters was located in the Temple Bar Building at 44 Court Street in the Brooklyn Heights neighborhood of Brooklyn.</field>
