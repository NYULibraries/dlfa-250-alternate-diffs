     <field name="bioghist_teim">Rolando Politi was born and raised in Naples, Italy and was involved with social work and housing throughout Europe, especially Germany, during the 1970s and 1980s. He came to New York in 1980 and moved between different squats. In April of 1984, Politi, along with several other people, took over 539 E. 13th Street. Politi was active in the East 13th Street Homestead Coalition and served as a representative at Community Board meetings.</field>
