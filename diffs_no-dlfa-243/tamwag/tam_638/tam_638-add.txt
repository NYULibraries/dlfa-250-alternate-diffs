     <field name="bioghist_teim">Peter Weiss (1925- ) is an attorney who specializes in international law in New York City. Born in Vienna, Austria in 1925, Weiss moved to the U.S. in 1941 and served in the United States Army from 1944 to 1946, and later in the Civilian Service of the U.S. Military Government in Berlin until 1947. He graduated from St. John's College in Annapolis in 1949 and later from Yale Law School in 1952. Weiss worked as a lawyer from 1955 to 2006 and has specialized in industrial property and international law since 1969. He has also served as a member of the governor's committee to review New York laws and procedures in the area of human rights (1967-1968), Vice Chairperson of the Lawyers Committee on American policy toward Vietnam (1969-1975), and as a member of the executive committee of the Fund for Peace (1981-1983). In 1968 he acted a delegate to the Democratic National Convention.</field>
