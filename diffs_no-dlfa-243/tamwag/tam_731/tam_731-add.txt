     <field name="scopecontent_teim">This collection consists of letters from 1941 to 1945 from David Smith to Esther Smith (nee Frank). They document David Smith's activities as an organizer in the United Electrical Workers in Cleveland, Ohio; his service in the United States Navy during World War II; and David and Esther's early relationship and marriage. This correspondence also documents the circumstances in which David was suspected of subversive activity by Naval Intelligence in between basic training and his naval service. There are also Communist Party USA (CPUSA) documents and pamphlets, as well as materials concerning anti-discrmination in housing, illegal evictions, and efforts to desegregate Stuyvesant Town from 1949 to 1958, including documents from the Town and Village Tenants' Committee to End Discrimination in Stuyvesant Town.</field>
