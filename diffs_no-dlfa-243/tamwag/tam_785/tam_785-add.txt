     <field name="bioghist_teim">Joanne Landy (1941-2017) was a writer, editor, and activist associated with the "Third Camp" socialist movement, who critiqued authoritarianism in nations on both sides of the Cold War. A student activist in the 1960s, Landy went on to be a longstanding member of the editorial board for the socialist magazine New Politics. In 1982, she co-founded the Campaign for Peace and Democracy, an international leftist solidarity movement advocating for egalitarian democratic socialism.</field>
