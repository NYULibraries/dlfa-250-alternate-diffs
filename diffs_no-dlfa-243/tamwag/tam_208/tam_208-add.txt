     <field name="bioghist_teim">Harry Turner (1919-1997), born in Brooklyn, N.Y., joined the Communist Party in the 1930s, left in the aftermath of the Soviet suppression of the Hungarian Revolution of 1956, became a Trotskyist, and was a member of the Socialist Workers Party (1964-66), then helped establish the Spartacist League, but left it in 1968, then participated in various U.S. and international Trotskyist organizations until his death. Turner was also a rehabilitation counseler, working for the New York State Office of Vocationa Rehabilitation, and an activist in the New York State Civil Service Employees Association. A detailed listing of Turner's writings is available at the Library.</field>
