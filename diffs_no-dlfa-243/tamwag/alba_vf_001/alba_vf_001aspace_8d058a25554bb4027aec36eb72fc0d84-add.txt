     <field name="scopecontent_teim">The Abraham Lincoln Brigade Archives (ALBA) is dedicated to preserving and communicating the history and memory of those Americans who participated in the Spanish Civil War on the Republican side ("volunteers"). It is also successor to the Veterans of the Abraham Lincoln Brigade. The website was first collected in 2008 and contained history of the war and the veterans organizations; dgitized documents, audio, images, and video related to the volunteers; a database of the volunteers with biographies; exhibit catalogs; online lesson plans for teaching about the Spanish Civil War; the San Francisco Monument; book reviews; events; ALBA in the news; George Watt Essay Contest; Teaching Institutes; and information about the ALBA collections at the Tamiment Library. Overtime, content on the website increased to include online resources of other institutions with Abraham Lincoln Brigade collections; a glossary; ALBA's Robeson in Spain comic book; publications on the Abraham Lincoln Brigade; fundraising efforts; ALBA/Puffin Award for Human Rights Activism; Human Rights Documentary Film Festival; obituaries; staff biographies; and the Susman lecture series. In early 2020, the website redesigned. Additional projects documented on the website include information on the Jarama Society; "Invisible Heroes: African Americans in the Spanish Civil War" documentary; the Perry Rosenstein Cultural Series; annual galas; and the Good Fight fundraising campaign.</field>
     <field name="scopecontent_teim">They also publish the quarterly journal, The Volunteer. Capture of the Volunteer's website began in 2010. Back issues of the Volunteer on the website date back to 1938.</field>
