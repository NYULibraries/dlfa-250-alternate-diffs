Little describes her childhood as split between Washington, where she was born, and the family's farm, which her mother inherited from her grandfather, who had been enslaved. She focuses on how she integrated with the family, noting that Mrs. Legette threatened to withdraw her membership from the local Presbyterian church if it did not allow Little to attend with the family.
