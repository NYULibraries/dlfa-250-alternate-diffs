-    <field name="abstract_teim">Miriam Nyhan is an Adjunct Assistant Professor of Irish Studies and Program Coordinator at New York University's Glucksman Ireland House. She received her BA and MPhil degrees from University College Cork (NUI) and her PhD from the European University Institute in Florence. Her research often focuses on oral history and Irish immigration patterns. This collection consists of 11 interviews conducted by  Nyhan in 2006 as part of her PhD research. She interviewed Irish immigrants, many of whom emigrated in the 1950s and settled in New York, to perform comparative research of Irish migration patterns.
