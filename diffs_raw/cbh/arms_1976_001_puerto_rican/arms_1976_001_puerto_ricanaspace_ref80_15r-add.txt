The daughter of farmers from the Puerto Rican countryside, she had limited educational opportunities and trained as a sewing machine operator. Rodríguez followed in the footsteps of an older sister and cousin by coming to New York, a move she says was made for the adventure rather than economic reasons. After first residing in the Bronx, she moved to Fulton Street in the Bedford-Stuyvesant neighborhood of Brooklyn.
