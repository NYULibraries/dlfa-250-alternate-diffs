     <field name="scopecontent_teim">The John Farnsworth Hammond, Jr. collection dates from 1899 to 1939, with the bulk of material dating from 1913 to 1915, and contains 98 items. The majority of the collection consists of a photograph album containing photographic prints and cyanotypes that document sewer construction projects in Brooklyn, Rochester, and Oswego, N.Y., as well as Mifflinburg, PA. Sewer construction photographs show street-level views, laborers, excavations, sewer pipes, dams, and construction equipment such as steam engines and horse-drawn tractors. Brooklyn photographs in the album originate in and around the Gowanus Canal, including the neighborhoods of Gowanus, Cobble Hill, and Carroll Gardens. Photographs in the album were taken by John Farnsworth Hammond, Jr., a contractor associated with the sewer construction projects, and V. Mayhew. Most construction scenes are identified by street name or neighborhood with a description of the image; photographs taken by V. Mayhew are labeled. The album also contains portraits of the John Farnsworth Hammond, Sr. family and rural scenes near their home in Newport, RI. </field>
