The bulk of the papers, dating from 1828 to 1847, are chiefly the papers of Henry Brevoort, Jr., his son James Carson Brevoort, and his grandson Henry L. Brevoort. A significant amount of the collection consists of correspondence, primarily letters from James Carson Brevoort to family members, chiefly to his parents.
