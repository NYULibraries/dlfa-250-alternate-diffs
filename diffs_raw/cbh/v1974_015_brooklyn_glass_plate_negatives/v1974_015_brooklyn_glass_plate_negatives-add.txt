The negatives are a mix of anonymous creations, reproductions, and original photography by Henry O. Korten. Images in the collection show rural Brooklyn life, street scenes, snowstorms, and the Brooklyn Navy Yard (formally known as the New York Naval Shipyard) in the late 19th century.
