     <field name="bioghist_teim">Rusty Zimmerman, thirty-eight at the time of the interview, is a White male who was born in Englewood, New Jersey. He and his family moved often during his early years, but Zimmerman relocated to New York City in 2005 in order to establish his trade as a freelance illustrator and painter. In 2009, he moved to the Crown Heights neighborhood of Brooklyn, and in an attempt to further hone his craft and engage his community, he began the Free Portrait Project in 2015, in which he interviewed and painted portraits of over 200 residents in Crown Heights over the course of one year. The culmination of the project was a series of exhibitions and community events, and Zimmerman received grant funding to edit and distribute the 900 hours of audio interviews he recorded while conducting the four-hour portrait sessions with each of his subjects.</field>
