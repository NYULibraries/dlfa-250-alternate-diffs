The Veterans of the Abraham Lincoln Brigade (VALB) adopted a constitution and a formal structure in 1939. In later years, VALB campaigned against Francisco Franco's regime in Spain, assisted refugees, and addressed other political issues, including the United States's foriegn policy in Cuba, Nicaragua, and Vietnam.
