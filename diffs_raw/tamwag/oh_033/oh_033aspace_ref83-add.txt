Between 1937 and 1955, the family lived in a three bedroom apartment on Madison Street in the LES. She earned her bachelor's degree in sociology and psychology from City College in Manhattan. Weinstein worked as a psychiatrist at Elmhurst Hospital in Queens. During her 20s, Weinstein travelled and worked in Israel for a few years, before returning to the US and working at Columbia-Presbyterian Clinic located in Upper Manhattan.
