The collection dates between 1966 and 1997, with the bulk of the material between 1980 and 1986. Topics include Irish Republican issues, in particular hunger strikes in Northern Ireland, human rights issues in Northern Ireland, and political prisoners in Northern Ireland and the United States, in particular Joe Doherty. Ephemera includes flyers, newsletters, bumper stickers, and posters from Irish and Irish-American organizations, including New York H-Block/Armagh Committee, American Irish Unity Committee, American Irish Heritage Foundation, Irish-American Defense Fund, Northern Ireland Civil Rights Association, Irish Northern Aid-IPOW Hunger Strike Defense Fund, and Sinn Fein. Government publications include two reports on hearings in the United States Senate, the first on the United States and United Kingdom Supplementary Extradition Treaty in 1985 and the second on Issues Relating to the Deportation of Michael O'Rourke in 1984. Audiovisual material includes audio recordings of three 1983 episodes of The Shamrock Shore, Ronald Reagan's 1984 speech to the Dáil Eireann, a 1984 Easter Rising commemoration, and other radio programs on Irish topics.</field>
