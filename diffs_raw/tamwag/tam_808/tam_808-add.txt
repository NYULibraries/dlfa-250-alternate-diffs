The school, located at 67 Stevenson Place in the Bronx, was a progressive residential center for young men and women who work during the day and study in the evening. The Susan E. Wilson Collection on the American Peoples School (dated 1933-1937) consists of materials collected by Wilson's parents documenting the culture and politics of the American Peoples School in the Bronx.
