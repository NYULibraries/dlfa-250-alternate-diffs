     <field name="bioghist_teim">Charles Fanning (1942- ) is a retired professor of Irish Studies. He earned his A.B. in English literature from Harvard University (1964) and his M.A. (1968) and Ph.D. (1972) from the University of Pennsylvania. Fanning taught at Bridgewater State College for 20 years and later at Southern Illinois University at Carbondale, where he formed an Irish Studies program.</field>
