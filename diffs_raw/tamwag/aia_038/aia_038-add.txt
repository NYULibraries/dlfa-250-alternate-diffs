     <field name="bioghist_teim">The Glucksman Ireland House, established in 1993, is the center for Irish Studies at New York University. It offers undergraduate and graduate courses in history, Irish language, literature, music, and politics. In addition to pursuing an undergraduate minor, a B.A./M.A., or an M.A. in Irish and Irish-American Studies, students at Glucksman Ireland House also have the ability to spend a summer studying abroad in Dublin, which is arranged through Glucksman Ireland House. When not convening classes, the Glucksman Ireland House also hosts weekly events and speakers on topics related to Irish politics, culture, and academia.</field>
