-    <field name="abstract_teim">Thomas "Tom" Moran (1944-2012) was born and raised in New York City in an Irish-American working-class family. As shop steward of Local 1-2 of the Utility Workers Union of America (UWUA), and later as safety director, Moran fought for workers' rights to a safe and healthy workplace, clashing with Con Edison over radiation exposure, asbestos, PCBs (polychlorinated biphenyls), clean air, lead, and other instances of worker exposure to toxic substances without warning. Involved in several New York State Supreme Court cases as a plaintiff on behalf of UWUA, Moran represented workers in their struggle for workplace protections, legal enforcement, and provisions for protective equipment and preventive training. This collection, spanning Moran's work from 1975 to 1993, contains court records, correspondence with government bodies and advocacy groups, technical manuals, health and safety training materials, and citations given to Con Edison by regulatory bodies. These materials were collected by Moran in the course of his work as Safety Director for the UWUA and represent the issues, cases, training materials, and litigation related to the workplace safety of UWUA members working for Con Edison.</field>
