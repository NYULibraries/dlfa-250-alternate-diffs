Tossio describes his childhood in Italy, noting that his education was cut short when his father fell ill and he had to work in order to support his family. He recounts his undocumented immigration to the US in January 1926; arriving in Baltimore, Maryland; and being assisted by local Italians on his way to New York. Tossio focuses on the relationship between the workers and the stevedore, noting that the stevedore was paid more money.
