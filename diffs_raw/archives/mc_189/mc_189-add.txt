     <field name="bioghist_teim">Albert Gallatin was born in Geneva, Switzerland in 1761. As a youth, he immigrated to the United States, where he quickly became involved in American politics. As a House of Representatives member he was the leader of the Democratic-Republican Party and served as Secretary of the Treasury from 1801 to 1813. Following his career as a politician and diplomat, Gallatin helped found New York University in 1831. The Gallatin School of Individualized Study is named in honor of his contributions to the University.</field>
