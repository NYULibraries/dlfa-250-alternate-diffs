     <field name="scopecontent_teim">The New York University Stern School of Business Collection of Pig Memorabilia contains approximately 571 three-dimensional objects. The majority of the collection consists of pig figurines of various sizes crafted from glass, porcelain, cloth, and more unconventional materials, such as soap and marzipan.   The objects in this collection vary in gender, color, size, place of origin, and sometimes patterns or markings.  The collection also includes utilitarian objects, such as piggy banks, salt and pepper shakers, ashtrays, gravy boats, pitchers, jewelry boxes, as well as memorabilia adorned with decorative pig motifs. The objects likely date between 1900 and 2013 and, as indicated by various stamps, labels, inscriptions, and crests, originate from the United States, parts of Europe and Asia.</field>
