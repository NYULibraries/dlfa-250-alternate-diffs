     <field name="scopecontent_teim">This collection spans a time period from 1758-1943 of the Astor family real estate business.  It includes deeds, leases, agreements, receipts, work orders, lease renewals, mortgages, ledgers, balances, daybooks, letterbooks (both personal and business), ticklers, and maps of Astor owned properties.  Individual documents in boxes are organized alphabetically, and volumes are organized alphabetically by person.  Oversize documents are organized in each folder alphabetically by location listed on the document.</field>
