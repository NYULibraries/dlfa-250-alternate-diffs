Marvel Shipbuilding Company (originally T.S. Marvel and Co.) was founded in 1884 by Thomas S. Marvel (1834-1916) and John Delaney in Newburgh, New York and operated until 1914. Each numbered entry in the ledger provides a boat's class, name, owner name, and hull dimensions, as well as contract amount and actual cost. Entry #107 records that the firm built J.P. Morgan's yacht "Corsair," actually Corsair III.
