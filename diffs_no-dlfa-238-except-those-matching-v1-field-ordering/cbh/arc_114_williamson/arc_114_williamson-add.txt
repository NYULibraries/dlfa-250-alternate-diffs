He emigrated from Holland around 1657 and settled in Gravesend, now a neighborhood in modern-day Brooklyn. Williamson and his wife Mayke Peterse Wyckoff (b. 1653) had seven children, the eldest of whom was Nicholas (b. 1680). Nicholas Williamson had at least 10 children, including son Rem (b. 1738).
