     <field name="bioghist_teim">Originally launched as  Wall Street Week with Louis Rukeyser in 1972, the show ran under this name from 1972 to 2002. During a revamp in 2002, original host, Louis Rukeyser, was replaced in an attempt to attract a younger audience. Relaunched as Wall Street Week with Fortune, it was now a joint venture between Fortune, Maryland Public Television, and PBS. The new hosts of the show were Geoffrey Colvin, the editorial director of Fortune, and Karen Gibbs, who was previously a senior business correspondent for Fox News. The host, along with panelists, would discuss financial markets, the economy, and financial issues in an attempt to help keep investors informed about developments. Despite attempts to grow the audience, the show was canceled in 2005.</field>
