     <field name="scopecontent_teim">This series includes approximately 670 audiocassette tapes, 457 of which have been digitized for access. None of the tapes were listened to during processing but the overall subject content is clear from the tape labels and overall collection context. The bulk of the tapes are of interviews that Carole Hyatt conducted in the 1980s and 1990s during her research for her books, both those realized and those contemplated but not realized or realized in a different form than originally planned. Most of these interviews were with individuals, but also included focus groups of men and/or women, couples, business partners, or other combinations. The bulk, reflecting the experiences of regular people, are with relatively unknown individuals, but some notable figures appear, such as newscaster Walter Cronkite, author William Shirer, boxer Gerry Cooney, former U.S. senator George McGovern, Vice-Presidential candidate Geraldine Ferraro, entrepreneur and founder of Famous Amos cookies Wally Amos, Apple co-founder Steve Wozniak, and New York University president and former Congressman John Brademas. </field>
