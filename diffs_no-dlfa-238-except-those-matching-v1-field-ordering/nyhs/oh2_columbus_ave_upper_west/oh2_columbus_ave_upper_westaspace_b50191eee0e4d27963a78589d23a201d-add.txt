In 1973, Medley moved into the apartment where she still lives, on Columbus Avenue at West 73rd Street. She describes her enjoyment of living somewhere racially diverse and describes the area as both "personable" and "dangerous." Medley discusses her career, starting as a receptionist and becoming a literary manager at the American Place Theater, and moving on to the Ensemble Studio Theatre where she became a playwright. Having retired as a faculty member in the Theatre Program at Sarah Lawrence College, Medley talks about her future plans and reflects on New York City's pivotal role in her career and life.</field>
