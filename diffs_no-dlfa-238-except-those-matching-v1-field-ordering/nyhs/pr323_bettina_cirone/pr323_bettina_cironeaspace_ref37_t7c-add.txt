     <field name="scopecontent_teim">This series contains mainly color slides, as well as some 4x6 color prints and a few negatives. In addition,there are photos documenting the filming of the movie The Great Gatsby in Trinity churchyard, as well as an American Indian Songfest held at Trinity, both in 1973.
