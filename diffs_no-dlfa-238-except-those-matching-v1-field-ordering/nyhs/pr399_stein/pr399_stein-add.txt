     <field name="bioghist_teim">Harry Stein (1891-1964) was born in Odessa, Ukraine in 1891, and as a child immigrated with his family to the U.S. The family appears in the 1900 census, living at 82 Eldridge Street on the Lower East Side of Manhattan. Stein and his brothers owned a chain of stores that sold sporting goods and camera equipment.
