     <field name="bioghist_teim">The family documented here descends chiefly from Monmouth Hart (1689–1761), of Westchester County, New York, and his wife, Sarah Ogden. Among the children of Monmouth and Sarah (Ogden) Hart was a son, Joseph Hart (circa 1726/7–1807). By his marriage to Elizabeth Gidney, Joseph was the father of another Joseph Hart (1757–1836). The younger Joseph married Tamar Budd, a daughter of Joseph Budd, and among their children was a son, Henry Hart, who married Esther Horton. The children of Henry and Esther (Horton) Hart included John Horton Hart (1819–1886), Joseph Budd Hart (1825–1878), Henry Azariah Hart (1832–1865), and George Washington Hart (1837–1896).</field>
