Todd, dry goods merchants at 12 Cedar, New York City) in 1934-1935. There is also one 1800 invoice and an unrelated 1858 invoice. In addition there are three other unrelated documents: A reprint version of the 1833 first number of the newspaper The Sun (the item is in fragments); an August 1841 transmittal letter from Munson I. Lockwood, Clerk of the Prison at Mount Pleasant, New York, (Sing Sing) to Comptroller John A. Collier at Albany regarding the monthly accounts, though the accounts themselves are not in the collection; and an undated, but likely circa 1831, letter from B. F. Williams to an addressee who appears to be Silas Wright, the New York state Comptroller. As the administrator of the estate of Benjamin Stocker of Salina, New York, Williams is seeking to settle a sale of real estate in Salina held by the estate.
