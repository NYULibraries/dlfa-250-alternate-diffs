     <field name="bioghist_teim">Milton Halsey Thomas (1903-1977) was a librarian, archivist, editor and bibliographer. He was born to Elijah and Elea Thomas on February 3, 1903 in  Troy, N.Y. Thomas studied at Tufts University before matriculating at Columbia University in 1926, where he earned his undergraduate degree while working as a librarian at the Butler Library of Philosophy. In 1929, Thomas became the curator of the Columbiana Collection, the institutional archives of Columbia University, a position he held for thirty years.   In 1959, Princeton University hired Thomas as their first-ever archivist, tasked with consolidating and organizing their institutional archives.</field>
