     <field name="bioghist_teim">Peter Curtenius was appointed Marshal of the District of New York by Thomas Jefferson in 1806. He served in this post until about the end of the War of 1812. Curtenius also monitored British prisoners of war and oversaw the U.S. District Court, District of New York.
