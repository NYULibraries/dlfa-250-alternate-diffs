-    <field name="bioghist_teim">Barnett W. (Russ) Hamberger (1940- ) held various positions at New York University since 1965, including Director of Branch Campus Affairs, Assistant to the Vice President for Academic Planning, and Assistant Vice Chancellor for Student Affairs. He received his bachelor's degree from Colgate University and M.A. from New York University. In the 1970s, after serving in various positions within the department of Academic Program Review, he became Associate Provost. While in this position, Hamberger reviewed new programs, served as liaison to the New York State Education Department on academic matters and to the University's regional accrediting agency. He also administered NYU's compliance with the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA) and managed staff who monitored academics and eligibility for NYU's athletics program. Hamberger also served as NYU's voting delegate to the University Athletic Association from its inception in 1985. He retired from his position in 2016.</field>
