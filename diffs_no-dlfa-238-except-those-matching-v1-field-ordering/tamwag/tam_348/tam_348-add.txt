These materials, shedding light on Flynn's life and political interests, were formerly in the possession of Peter Martin, the son of Carlo Tresca and Elizabeth Gurley Flynn's sister, Bina. This small collection of documents, clippings and memorabilia was apparently gathered together by Flynn's nephew, Peter Martin, shortly after her death. Peter Martin (1922-1988) was the son of Carlo Tresca and Elizabeth Gurley Flynn's sister, Bina, who later married Romulo Bobba and became the mother of Roberta Bobba.</field>
