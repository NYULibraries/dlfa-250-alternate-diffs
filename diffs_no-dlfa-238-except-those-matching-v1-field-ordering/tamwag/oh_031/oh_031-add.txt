     <field name="scopecontent_teim">The Oral Histories series is comprised of sixty-three interviews that were conducted between 1980 and 1988 by Andrew Horn of the Tamiment Playhouse Archives and by NYU Performance Studies graduate student Martha S. LoMonaco for her dissertation and subsequent book on the Tamiment Playhouse, Every Week a Broadway Revue. Of note in the collection are interviews with Woody Allen, Carol Burnett, Imogene Coca, and Neil Simon. Materials in the Shows series were recorded live on reel to reel audiotape at the Tamiment Playhouse from 1955-1960.</field>
