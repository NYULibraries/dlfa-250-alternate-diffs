     <field name="bioghist_teim">Rose Rubin was born in Bialystok, in the Polish region of the Russian Empire in 1889.  She worked in a union textile factory before her immigration to the United States (US) in 1907.  In the US she worked in the garment industry and in a handkerchief factory.  She married her first husband in 1908 and had one child with him before his death in 1910.  Her second husband had two children from a previous marriage, and they had three children together.  After his death, Rubin returned to work in the garment industry.</field>
