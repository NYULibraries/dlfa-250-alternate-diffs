     <field name="bioghist_teim">Edward Reid was born near Montego Bay, Jamaica on April 22, 1892.  He had one older brother.  His father worked as a shipwright, traveling aboard ships and performing repairs as necessary, and his mother worked in a laundry.  He attended Mt. Zion School in Jamaica until he was 14 years old, when he trained in carpentry.  He immigrated to the United States (US) in 1919 and lived with his cousin in Charleston, South Carolina for two years before migrating to New York, New York. He worked as a carpenter for a number of employers, including the Standard Oil Company and the Consolidated Gas Company in Charleston.  He joined Local 52 of an unspecified union of Black carpenters; the Odd Fellows; and the Freemasons while in the US.</field>
