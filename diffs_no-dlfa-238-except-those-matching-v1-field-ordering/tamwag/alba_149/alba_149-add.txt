     <field name="bioghist_teim">Maury Colow (1917- ) was born in Brooklyn. In the 1930s, Colow was a member of the Young Communist League and the Communist Party. He joined the Abraham Lincoln Brigades and arrived in Spain on February 17, 1937, serving with the 86 BD, 20th BN, Co. 2. He returned to the United States in 1938 and later served in the US Navy during World War II. Upon his return he worked in the Midwest as an organizer for the Congress of Industrial Workers (CIO) affiliated International Fur &amp; Leather Workers Union. In the late 1960s, Colow served as the National Executive Secretary for the Veterans of the Abraham Lincoln Brigade.</field>
