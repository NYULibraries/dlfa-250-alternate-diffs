     <field name="bioghist_teim">Abraham Nash (1915-1996) is a former labor leader and professor of sociology at Cornell University. Nash grew up in New York City and received his first job through the Works Progress Administration for a theater group and managing the Labor Bookstore in Greenwich Village. He later worked as a shop steward for the United Automobile Workers at an airplane plant in Long Island. Nash was drafted during World War II and served with the Army in Europe. Following the war, Nash resumed his involvement with labor organizations working with the United Automobile Workers in Detroit and a number of unions in New York including the United Electrical Workers Union, the District Council 37 of the American Federation of State, County and Municipal Employees, the Taxi Drivers Union, the musicians union and Local 1199 of the hospital workers union.</field>
