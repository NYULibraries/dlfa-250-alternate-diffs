-    <field name="abstract_teim">Julius "Julie" Wiesenfeld (1913-1979) was a professor of mathematics and union and civil rights advocate. While in Cedar Falls, Wiesenfeld served as the President of the Black Hawk County Union Council, and was active in union, civil rights, and anti-war organizing, helping to organize the UNI chapter of the American Federation of Teachers, and adovcating for amnesty of Vietnam draft resistance while he was a delegate to the State Democratic Convention in 1968 and 1972. The Julius Wiesenfeld Papers (dated approximately 1941-1977) consist of correspondence, writings, and newspaper clippings on a variety of topics related to the Old Left, including labor strikes, union organizing, conflict within the Socialist Labor Party of America, voting rights and voting registration efforts in the 1960s, opposition to American involvement in Vietnam, Marxist theory and history, and international Marxism during the mid-20th century, including the student-led Red Guards in China and de-Stalinisation in the Soviet Union.</field>
