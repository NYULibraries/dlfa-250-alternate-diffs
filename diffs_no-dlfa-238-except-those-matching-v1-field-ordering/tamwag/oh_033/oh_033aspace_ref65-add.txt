     <field name="bioghist_teim">Edward Muse was born in 1921 in Danville, Virginia and was one of seven children.  His mother worked as a teacher before her children were born.  His father was a railway clerk for the United States Postal Service and established the first bank for African-Americans in Virginia in 1921.  Muse migrated to New York in 1938 and settled in the Harlem neighborhood of Manhattan, New York and lived in the Lower East Side neighborhood of Manhattan between 1948 and 1954.  He served in the United States Army during World War II before attending Columbia University.  He worked at the WMCA radio station in New York, at the Hebrew Immigrant Aid Society, and as a staff director for the National Association for the Advancement of Colored People.</field>
