     <field name="bioghist_teim">Simon Shulman was born in 1900 in the Lower East Side (LES) neighborhood of Manhattan, New York.  His family was Jewish and he had two siblings. He graduated from Oswego State College in Oswego, New York  He worked as a commercial artist creating posters for the Academy of Music on Fourteenth Street in Manhattan and other theatres in New York City and taught commercial art at George Westinghouse Vocational and Technical High School in Brooklyn.
